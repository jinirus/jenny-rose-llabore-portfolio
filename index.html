About Me

I am an EA Mentor and Trainer at Atlas Assistants, working at the intersection of people development, operational excellence, and client success. My role is multifaceted — part coach, part trainer, part systems thinker — and every piece of it is oriented around one goal: helping Executive Assistants become indispensable partners to the clients they serve.

I lead with empathy, but I hold high standards. I believe in building systems that are both rigorous and kind — frameworks that challenge people to grow while making sure they never feel alone in the process. Every SOP I write, every coaching conversation I have, and every training session I run is grounded in that belief.

My current focus areas include EA performance management, batch training evaluation, and recruitment system design — building the infrastructure that helps Atlas EAs thrive at every stage of their careers.

What I Do

🎓

Training & Skill Development

I design and deliver training programmes that help EAs build the competencies they need to thrive — from Day 1 onboarding through to advanced performance levels. I evaluate EA readiness across dimensions like conceptual understanding, simulation output, participation quality, and technical readiness.

🤝

Mentoring & Coaching

I mentor EAs one-on-one to bridge the gap between where they are and where they need to be. When an EA is struggling — whether with skills, confidence, or client dynamics — I step in with a coaching lens to identify root causes and co-create a path forward.

🔧

Client Partnership Recovery

When client relationships hit a roadblock, I work to diagnose what's going wrong and design targeted interventions — including structured recovery plans (such as Elevate and Pre-Elevate Plans) that are both rigorous and human-centred.

📊

Operational Systems Design

I build the frameworks and SOPs that underpin how Atlas manages EA performance at scale — including tiered performance management systems, EAST Alignment Flag protocols, EA Pulse Survey pipelines, and Agile sprint-based improvement cycles.

🧩

Recruitment & Talent Pipeline

I contribute to shaping how Atlas identifies and onboards new EA talent — designing recruitment systems that evaluate not just skills, but culture fit and long-term potential.
